About us

From fallen tree to finished piece

Blessed Woods started with wood that had already been cut down — branches trimmed, trees removed, offcuts left behind. Rather than let it go to waste, we save the pieces worth saving.

Each block is cleaned, cut down to size, and carved by hand into something meant to last: a house number, a keepsake, a small stopper for a door that won't stay shut. The grain, the knots, the small imperfections — they stay. That's the wood's own history, not a flaw to sand away.

  • Reclaim

    We collect wood from trees that have already come down.

  • Cut

    Larger pieces are cut down into workable blocks.

  • Carve

    Each block is shaped and carved entirely by hand.

  • Finish

    A final sand and oil finish brings out the grain.
